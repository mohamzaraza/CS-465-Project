# Project Motivation:
#  Why are we doing it ?
#   A number of people want to get into fitness but are clueless on how to start, how to perform certain exercises with correct form, how to structure their workouts properly and etc. LiftLogic aims to solve these problems by features such as, video demonstration of the exercises, A calorie counter and an AI powered fitness assistant. Another reason why are we doing it is because we have the knowledge to do so, and think that it would be a great idea, it is not revolutionary but similar apps often get you paywalled after the first questionnaire.
#  Who will it benefit?
#    Anyone trying to improve their health without paying a trainer.  This app helps anyone ranging from students looking to get deeply indulged inot fitness to a total begineer who just wants to better their lifestyle.
